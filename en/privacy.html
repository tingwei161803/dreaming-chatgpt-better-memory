Deletion & retention 2

Memory ≠ a single chat

Dreaming synthesizes memory in the background from many conversations, so deleting one chat doesn't necessarily remove what was already learned. Manage memory directly via the summary / settings.

Security 2

Personalization is persistent

When personal context is carried into every chat, be mindful about what you share — and review the summary periodically so nothing unexpected sticks around.

Prompt injection is a known category

Generally, memory + untrusted content is a discussed risk area: malicious instructions in third-party material could try to influence a model. Be cautious with content from unknown sources. (General context, not specific to this release.)

Accuracy 2

It can still be wrong

Even improved, models can misremember or confabulate. Notably, in the article the model re-verifies facts against real sources before answering — verify anything important yourself.

Context can resurface

Personalization is a double-edged sword: something mentioned earlier may shape a later answer unexpectedly. Use a Temporary Chat when you want a clean slate.

Region & policy 2

Availability varies by region

Rollout is US Plus/Pro first, with more countries and Free/Go to follow. Memory availability has historically differed by region — check what's available where you are.

Mind local data rules

Persistent personalization touches data-protection regimes (e.g. GDPR-style rights around personal data and erasure). Review your settings and regional terms before relying on it. (General context.)
